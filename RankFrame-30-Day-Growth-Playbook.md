## Part 1 — Google Business Profile (biggest local lever)

For local keywords and the map pack, your Google Business Profile (GBP) matters more than your website. Do this first.

### Setup & verification
- Create/claim your profile at **business.google.com**. Verify (video or postcard) — nothing ranks until verified.
- Business name = your real name exactly (no keyword stuffing — it's against Google's rules and can get you suspended).
- Address: use your real address, or set a **service area** if you go to customers instead of them coming to you.

### Optimization checklist
- **Primary category** = the single most accurate one (e.g. "SEO agency", "Dentist", "Auto repair shop"). Add secondary categories for other services.
- Fill **every** field: hours (incl. holiday hours), phone, website, services, and a keyword-natural business description.
- Add **10+ real photos**: storefront, interior, team, work/products. Listings with 5+ photos get ~3× the engagement. Add new photos monthly.
- Turn on **messaging** and **Q&A** — seed 3–5 common questions and answer them yourself.
- Post a **Google Update** weekly (offer, tip, news). Active profiles rank better.

### NAP consistency (do this everywhere)
Your **N**ame, **A**ddress, **P**hone must be **identical** on your website, GBP, Apple, Bing, and every directory below. Inconsistent NAP is the #1 reason local listings underperform. Decide the exact format once (e.g. "Suite 200" vs "Ste 200") and use it everywhere.

## Part 3 — Reviews (ranking factor + conversion)

Reviews are a top-3 local ranking factor and the thing buyers look at before calling. **Only ever request reviews from real customers. Never buy or fake them** — Google detects and penalizes it, and it can get your listing removed.

### The system
1. Ask **every** happy customer, right after you've delivered value (that's when they're most willing).
2. Send them your **Google review short link** (get it in GBP → "Ask for reviews").
3. Follow up once if they don't respond. Don't nag.
4. **Respond to every review** — thank the positive ones, calmly address the negative ones. Response rate is itself a signal.
5. Aim for a steady trickle (e.g. 2–4/month) rather than 20 at once, which looks unnatural.

### Copy-paste templates

**Email / text — first ask:**
> Hi [Name] — it was a pleasure working with you on [project/service]. If you have 30 seconds, a quick Google review would mean a lot and helps other [local] folks find us: [your review link]. Thank you!

**Follow-up (once, ~4 days later):**
> Hi [Name], just following up in case you missed my note — if you were happy with [service], a short Google review really helps us out: [your review link]. No worries if you're busy. Thanks again!

**Responding to a positive review:**
> Thanks so much, [Name] — it was great working with you on [service]. We really appreciate you taking the time, and we're here whenever you need us.

**Responding to a negative review (stay calm, never argue):**
> Hi [Name], thank you for the feedback and I'm sorry [X] didn't meet expectations. I'd like to make it right — please reach me directly at [email/phone] so we can sort this out.

## Part 4 — Backlinks & digital PR (the new-domain bottleneck)

Links from other websites are the strongest ranking signal and the hardest to get — which is exactly why they move the needle for a new domain. **No bought links, no PBNs, no spam.** These get you penalized. Earn them instead:

### Fastest wins first
- **The citations in Part 2** are links — start there.
- **Supplier / partner / association links:** any vendor, partner, chamber of commerce, or trade association you belong to. Ask for a listing or a link. These are easy and high-trust.
- **Local chamber of commerce** + local business associations — usually a members directory link.
- **Testimonials for tools you use:** write a short testimonial for software/vendors you genuinely use; they often publish it with a link back.

### Ongoing (5–10 links/month is a strong pace)
- **HARO / Connectively / Qwoted / Featured.com:** reporters request expert quotes daily. Answer 2–3 relevant SEO/local-marketing queries a week; when quoted you get a link from a real publication. This is the single best white-hat link source for a service business.
- **Guest posts:** pitch genuinely useful articles to local business blogs, industry sites, and marketing publications. Lead with value, not "can I get a link."
- **Original data / linkable assets:** your "30+ audits" post is one. More data studies, free checklists, or a simple free tool earn links passively over time. (I can build these on the site whenever you want.)
- **Competitor backlink gap:** find who links to competitors (Ahrefs/Semrush free tools or trials) and pitch the same sources.

### Outreach templates

**Partner / supplier link ask:**
> Hi [Name] — we work with [Company] regularly and recommend you often. Would you be open to adding us to your partners/clients page? Here's our info: [Business name, URL]. Happy to return the favor with a listing on our site.

**Guest post pitch:**
> Hi [Name], I read your piece on [topic] and had a thought for a follow-up your readers would find useful: "[specific, useful title]." I run [business] and can write it exclusively for you — no fluff, genuinely actionable. Want me to send an outline?

**HARO / reporter response (keep it tight, quotable):**
> [2–3 sentence expert answer with a concrete number or example.] — [Your Name], [Title] at RankFrame SEO ([URL]). Happy to expand or provide data.

## The 4-week calendar

**Week 1 — Foundation**
- Claim & fully optimize Google Business Profile (Part 1)
- Claim Apple Business Connect + Bing Places
- Submit the 10 core citations (Part 2)
- Set your exact NAP format and record it

**Week 2 — Citations + reviews engine**
- Submit industry-specific directories (5/day)
- Turn on the review system; ask your last 10 happy customers (Part 3)
- Respond to any existing reviews
- Send me your first real testimonials/photos (Part 5)

**Week 3 — Links**
- Get partner/supplier/chamber links (Part 4 fast wins)
- Sign up for HARO/Connectively; answer 3 queries
- Draft 1 guest-post pitch

**Week 4 — Momentum + measure**
- Keep review asks going (2–4/month ongoing)
- Answer more HARO queries; send guest-post pitch
- In **Google Search Console → Performance**, check **Average position** on your target keywords — you should start seeing terms appear
- Re-submit sitemap.xml in GSC; Request indexing on your top pages

Then repeat Weeks 3–4 monthly. Reviews + links + fresh content, compounding.
